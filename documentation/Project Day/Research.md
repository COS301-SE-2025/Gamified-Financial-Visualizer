# 📚 Research Contribution & Background

## 1. Introduction

This document outlines the **research background** and **contributions** made throughout the development of the **Gamified Financial Visualizer (GFV)** system.  
Our aim was to explore existing technologies, frameworks, and methodologies to identify innovative ways of combining **financial literacy**, **gamification**, and **AI-driven insights** into a cohesive digital experience.

---

## 2. Background Research

### 2.1 Financial Literacy and Gamification
Research focused on understanding how **gamification** improves **user engagement** and **learning outcomes** in financial education.  
Key findings included:
- Gamification increases retention and motivation by integrating rewards, levels, and progress systems.  
- XP-based systems encourage consistent participation and provide measurable growth indicators.  
- Similar approaches were observed in tools like **Duolingo (education)** and **Monument Valley (habit formation)**, which inspired the system’s progression structure.
- The main real life competitor being Vault22 & MoneyManager, which lacked the gamification needs.
---

### 2.2 AI in Personal Finance
We examined how **machine learning** and **natural language processing** can enhance personal finance management by automating insights.  
This included:
- Researching **transaction classification models** (DistilBERT, RoBERTa, and FinancialBERT).  
- Studying open datasets on spending patterns and classification approaches.  
- Implementing a **custom FastAPI AI service** for real-time financial insight generation.

**Contribution:**  
The project fine-tuned a lightweight **DistilBERT-based classifier** capable of labeling financial transactions into meaningful categories with high accuracy, contributing to the emerging field of **AI-assisted financial literacy**.
This model is public on Hugging Face such that others can benefit from its development 

---

### 2.3 3D & AR Visualization in Education
Research into **interactive visualization** methods revealed that **3D cityscapes** and **augmented reality environments** enhance comprehension of abstract financial data.  
Our work explored:
- **React Three Fiber** and **Three.js** for immersive data storytelling.  
- Techniques for scene optimization, lighting realism, and AR asset deployment (USDZ/GLB).  
- Application of spatial metaphors (buildings = financial domains) to support cognitive association.

## 3. Project’s Research Contribution

The **Gamified Financial Visualizer** contributes to academic and applied research in the following ways:

| Area | Contribution |
|------|---------------|
| **Gamified Financial Learning** | Introduces an XP-based tier progression system to motivate continuous financial education. |
| **AI-driven Insights** | Deploys an explainable AI classifier for real-time categorization of user transactions and spending analysis. |
| **3D Visualization of Finance** | Pioneers the use of a 3D city metaphor for visualizing budgets, goals, and achievements. |
| **Cross-Platform Financial Engagement** | Integrates React (web), React Native (mobile), and FastAPI (AI service) into one unified gamified ecosystem. |
| **Academic Insight** | Demonstrates how gamification principles from cognitive science can be directly applied to personal finance education. |

---

## 4. Methodology Overview

1. **Literature Review:** Conducted a multi-disciplinary review across finance, education technology, and game design.
2. **Prototype Development:** Built multiple experimental prototypes to test learning retention, user motivation, and engagement levels.
3. **AI Evaluation:** Conducted iterative experiments to optimize model accuracy, classification latency, and explainability.
4. **User-Centered Design:** Implemented persona-driven UX informed by the research findings.

---

## 5. Future Research Directions

The project lays the foundation for further studies in:
- Adaptive financial learning systems using **reinforcement learning** and **RAG**.
- Personalized feedback generation using **explainable AI (XAI)**.
- Integration of **neuro-gamification** (emotionally adaptive game design).
- Real-time multiplayer functionality of the game using a game engine in the cloud.
- Longitudinal studies measuring **financial behavior change** through gamified systems.
